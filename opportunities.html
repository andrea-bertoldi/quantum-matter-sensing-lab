Opportunities

We welcome inquiries from motivated students and postdoctoral researchers interested in cold atoms, quantum sensing, collective light–matter physics, and programmable physical systems.

Research topics in the group include atom interferometry for gravity and fundamental physics, continuous ultracold-atom sources, optical clocks and superradiance, cavity and waveguide QED, hollow-core-fiber experiments, numerical tools for cold-atom experiments, and programmable platforms for emergent physics.

Experimental, numerical, electronics, and FPGA backgrounds can all be relevant depending on the project.

Prospective candidates are encouraged to contact Andrea Bertoldi with a CV and a short description of their scientific interests.
